# Sample book script (the `is_demo` audiobook)

Spoken onboarding guide that ships as every user's first library item. Narrate
with a premium Multilingual voice so it doubles as a voice-quality demo. Convert
on the owner account, flag `is_demo = TRUE`, unflag the old classic demo row.

~180 words · ~75 sec spoken. Action-first — hook, then the two source paths,
then how conversion works, then listening.

---

Welcome to EbookVoice — let's turn your first book into audio.

To make the most of the app, start by getting an ebook onto your device. You can
download one from anywhere you get your books — your own ebook collection,
BookShare, or a free-classics site like Project Gutenberg or Standard Ebooks. Or
skip the hunt entirely: tap the Discover tab and pick from our built-in library
of around seventy-five thousand classics — from Pride and Prejudice to Sherlock
Holmes — all free to convert.

We read three kinds of file: standard ebook files, Word documents, and Daisy
books — the accessible format used by BookShare and similar libraries.

<!-- Note: acronyms (EPUB / .docx / DAISY-in-caps) are deliberately avoided in
     the spoken text. When a voice emits no WordBoundary events the worker falls
     back to evenly-distributed synthetic timings, and a letter-spelled acronym
     ("E-P-U-B") takes far longer to say than its even slice — desyncing
     follow-along for the rest of the chapter. The exact file extensions live in
     /guide and on the Upload screen instead. -->


Once you've got your book, tap upload, pick the file, and choose a voice — over
two hundred of them, across twenty-seven languages. EbookVoice reads the whole
thing aloud, chapter by chapter. You can close the app while it converts; it keeps
going, and your audiobook appears in your library when it's ready.

Then just press play. Listening is always free — background play, lock-screen
controls, follow-along highlighting, every voice and every language. Ready? Grab
a book, and let's begin.
